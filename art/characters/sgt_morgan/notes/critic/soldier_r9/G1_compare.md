# Round 9: ChatGPT (G1) beside the three Claude critics

G1 is ChatGPT (GPT-6, Codex), asked through `gen/REQUESTS.md` R006 to review the same packet blind, with all
three lenses at once. It scored overall 4 (Q1-Q6: 6, 5, 4, 4, 5, 5), with 22 located defects, one of them
blocking. The Claude critics scored 6, 6 and 4 (median 6). G1 is not part of the tally; `tally.py` takes only
C1-C3.

**Both sides found** the boots' toe (galosh or wedge), the forearm guards (cone, drum or cylinder stack), the
right pauldron high and off the shoulder, the clinging seat, the leg rig as boxes, the plain carrier front,
the knee caps as boxy cups, the loose left-hand wrap, the hair as a cap with spikes, and the near-black upper
trousers.

**Only G1 found:**
- **D1 (blocking; checked, real).** At the right elbow (`pair_turn_side_R.png` [201, 322, 258, 358]), the
  elbow pad's upper strap is broken into floating pieces, with a pinched crease in the sleeve under it. This is
  armour geometry, the strap cut where it meets the sleeve. It shows in the side view only.
- **D2.** Both carrier shoulder pads bridge over open gaps above the shoulders, as round 8's m29 also found.
- **D14.** The belt is a rigid hoop that stands off the body at the back, matching the stand-off report's
  7.0 mm. The belt band uses the waist's widest section everywhere.
- **D3, D13.** The soft kit reads as hard boxes: magazine pouches with flat lids and a suitcase-like rear bag.
  G1 ranks this first, and its one change would be to rebuild the carrier and pouches as fabric over the real
  plates and magazines.
- **D8.** The left upper arm is a straight padded column, without the taper from shoulder to elbow.
- **D21.** The knee faces have smeared vertical highlights and pooled dark lower edges, so they look melted.
- **D22.** The gaiter has a thick open upper lip and evenly stacked folds.

**What the Claude critics weighted that G1 did not:** the armour reading as pale plastic, the waxy face (G1
excluded it as declared), the foil-like shin lumps, and the bright upper-arm bands.

The different model family paid off. G1 judges form and contact more than colour, and it found the one broken
piece of geometry the Claude critics missed.
